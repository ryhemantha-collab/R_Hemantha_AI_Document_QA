Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences is a private university located in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.
The university was established in 2013.
The university includes academic schools and departments that offer undergraduate and postgraduate programmes.
The Faculty of Engineering and Technology includes the Department of Computer Science and Engineering.
The Bachelor of Technology programme develops technical knowledge, problem solving ability, and professional skills.
Artificial Intelligence is a field of computer science concerned with systems that perform tasks associated with intelligent behaviour.
